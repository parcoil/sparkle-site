## 2. Open Source & Data

Sparkle is free and open source you are allowed to:

- Study the source code

Sparkle Does not sell your data. We also dont store your data.

Sparkle uses [Posthog](https://posthog.com/) to track how many installs and catch bugs in the frontend. We do not use or sell your data.

Older versions of sparkle uses [Sentry](https://sentry.io/) For Error catching.
